# ManhwaTracker
An extension for tracking manhwa chapters, with cloud for seamless device shifts.

THE EXTENSION:
Come back to a series months later and have no idea which chapter you were on? Manhwa Tracker remembers for you, on any site, automatically.

Just read. The extension notes the chapter you're on and keeps it in a clean library, so you can jump straight back in.

WENT BACK TO CHECK A PANEL? NO PROBLEM
Every chapter you open is kept in a reading history for that series. Jump back to chapter 9 to check something and your place can stay at chapter 20. Choose whether "your place" means the furthest chapter you've read or the last one you opened, globally or per series.

FEATURES
• Works on any manhwa, manga or webtoon site, with no site lists to wait for
• Fully automatic: no buttons to press while you read
• Library with covers and search
• Reading history dropdown for every series (up to 300 chapters each)
• Continue, Next and Latest buttons to jump straight in
• Optional new-chapter alerts when a series you follow posts a new chapter
• Optional sync across devices with a free account
• Undo for accidental removals, plus a Removed list that keeps series for 30 days
• Per-site controls: block or allow sites. YouTube and social sites are ignored by default
• Edit a chapter by hand whenever you want
• Pause tracking at any time
• Export and import your library

PRIVACY
Your library and history are stored on your device. If you turn on sync, your series list (including history) is stored in your own account so it can follow you between devices. No ads, no analytics, and your data is never sold.

HOW IT WORKS
Manhwa Tracker recognises chapter numbers in page addresses and titles, waits a few seconds to be sure you're actually reading, then saves your place.

Manhwa Tracker is an independent project and is not affiliated with any reading site.'
